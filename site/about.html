About The Bellevue Homes

A designer-led residential development team creating refined custom homes for modern city living in Chicago.

The Bellevue Homes is a Chicago luxury residential development and custom homebuilding team creating refined homes for modern city living. We believe a home is more than a place to live—it is a sanctuary, an experience, and a reflection of the life you choose to lead. Guided by an award-winning team renowned for artistry in residential design, we craft living spaces that are as unique as the people who call them home.

Each Bellevue home is a one-of-a-kind composition, where elegance and comfort coexist effortlessly. With an impeccable eye for spatial harmony, our team has spent years reimagining how interiors flow—how light moves through a room, how transitions between spaces feel natural, seamless, and serene. Every element is intentional: a balance of refined minimalism and inviting warmth, creating interiors that are both visually stunning and deeply livable.

Our homes are distinguished by their clean, modern aesthetic. The Bellevue home is designed to enrich daily life. With an unrivaled dedication to craftsmanship, our team’s work has earned widespread acclaim, continually setting new standards for residential design in Chicago’s most sought-after neighborhoods, from Lakeview to Lincoln Square.

At Bellevue Homes, we don’t just build homes—we create spaces that resonate, inspire, and endure. See the results in our design gallery and hear from homeowners, brokers, and designers who know the work firsthand.
