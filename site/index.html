The Bellevue Homes is a design-led residential development and custom homebuilding company creating refined custom estates, bespoke residences, and transformative renovations in Chicago. Guided by architectural detail, thoughtful spatial flow, and elevated craftsmanship, our homes are shaped to feel distinctive, livable, and enduring.

Led by Anita Goyal, Designer and Real Estate Developer, The Bellevue Homes brings together development discipline and a designer’s eye for light, flow, proportion, and warmth. Explore our Chicago portfolio projects, browse the luxury home design gallery, or contact The Bellevue Homes to begin a conversation.

The Bellevue Lifestyle

Three ways we shape refined city living

Custom Estates

Custom Estates are complete residences shaped around refined city living, generous layouts, and a clear sense of architectural balance.

Each estate is approached as a full residential composition, with thoughtful transitions, intuitive flow, and carefully considered details that support everyday comfort.

View featured Chicago projects

Bespoke Residences

Bespoke Residences are one-of-a-kind homes composed around spatial flow, material selections, and the daily experience of living beautifully.

The work is design-led and detail focused, balancing architectural harmony with spaces that feel warm, functional, and deeply personal.

Browse the design gallery

Transformations

Transformations reimagine existing residences through the same design-led lens used for new custom homes and bespoke residential development.

Each renovation is considered through flow, proportion, comfort, and refined materials so the home feels renewed while remaining deeply livable.
