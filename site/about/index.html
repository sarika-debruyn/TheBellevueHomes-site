About The Bellevue Homes

A designer-led residential development team creating refined custom homes for modern city living in Chicago.

The Bellevue Homes is a Chicago luxury residential development and custom homebuilding team creating refined homes for modern city living. We believe a home is more than a place to live: it is a sanctuary, an experience, and a reflection of the life you choose to lead. Every Bellevue residence is designer-led from the first sketch, shaping living spaces that are as unique as the people who call them home.

Each Bellevue home is a one-of-a-kind composition, where elegance and comfort coexist effortlessly. Our approach centers on spatial harmony: how light moves through a room, how transitions between spaces feel natural, seamless, and serene. Every element is intentional: a balance of refined minimalism and inviting warmth, creating interiors that are both visually stunning and deeply livable.

Five residences across Lakeview and Lincoln Square make up the published Bellevue portfolio, ranging from 4,400 to 6,200 square feet, three completed and two underway. Each is documented in full, including square footage, program, and status, so the work speaks for itself rather than in adjectives.

At Bellevue Homes, we don’t just build homes; we create spaces that resonate, inspire, and endure. See the results in our design gallery and hear directly from homeowners, brokers, and designers who know the work firsthand.

Anita Goyal

Designer and Real Estate Developer

Anita Goyal leads The Bellevue Homes, overseeing every published residence from initial design through completion. Her practice pairs development discipline (budget, sequencing, delivery) with a designer’s eye for light, flow, proportion, and warmth, the same principles described firsthand by the designers, brokers, and homeowners who have worked with her.
