Lincoln Square Classic Residence

Lincoln Square, Chicago · Bespoke Residence

Rendering

This completed Lincoln Square residence balances classic proportion with the way families actually live: 5,500 square feet, six bedrooms, and five and a half baths composed around light, sight lines, and easy movement between rooms. The white exterior reads timeless on its tree-lined street, while inside, the Bellevue design language carries through — custom cabinetry, warm neutral materials, and baths finished to a spa standard. It sits in good company: the 6,200-square-foot Grand Residence is complete nearby, and The Oakdale House is now under construction in the same neighborhood.
