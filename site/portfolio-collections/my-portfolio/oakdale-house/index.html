The Oakdale House

Lincoln Square, Chicago · Custom Estate

Rendering

The Oakdale House is the newest Bellevue residence underway in Lincoln Square: 5,620 square feet, six bedrooms, and five and a half baths behind a composed brick façade and stately front entry. The program follows the principles that shaped our completed Lincoln Square homes — generous shared rooms at the center of the plan, natural transitions between spaces, and finishes selected with a designer’s discipline. To see where the standard is set, walk through the completed Grand Residence and Classic Residence nearby, or browse the design gallery.
