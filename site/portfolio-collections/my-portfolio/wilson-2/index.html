Lincoln Square Grand Residence

Lincoln Square, Chicago · Custom Estate

At 6,200 square feet, this is the largest residence in the published Bellevue portfolio — a completed Lincoln Square estate with seven bedrooms and five and a half baths. The scale allowed the plan to breathe: broad shared rooms for entertaining, distinct family zones, and suites that feel private without feeling far away. A broker who sold in the neighborhood called a Bellevue Lincoln Square home “one of the most beautiful homes I have had the pleasure of selling”. Its completed neighbor, the Lincoln Square Classic Residence, shows the same standard at 5,500 square feet.
