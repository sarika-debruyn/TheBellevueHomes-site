Lakeview Modern Residence

Lakeview, Chicago · Bespoke Residence

This completed Lakeview residence puts the Bellevue design language on full display: crisp white massing, tall black-framed windows that pull daylight deep into the plan, and 4,400 square feet arranged around calm, connected living. Six bedrooms and four and a half baths give the home unusual flexibility for a city lot, while the open main level moves naturally from cooking to gathering to quiet. The interiors carry the finish standard you can study in our design gallery — custom cabinetry, considered lighting, and spa-caliber baths. A sibling estate is under construction nearby in Lakeview.
