Lakeview Custom Home

Lakeview, Chicago · Custom Estate

Now rising in Lakeview, this 4,400-square-foot custom estate pairs a stately gray brick exterior with the composed, light-driven interiors The Bellevue Homes is known for. The six-bedroom program is arranged for real family life: generous shared spaces at the heart of the plan, private suites above, and four and a half baths finished to the spa standard seen throughout our design gallery. Like every Bellevue residence, it is designer-led from the first drawing — proportion, flow, and material warmth decided long before ground was broken. Completed Bellevue homes in Lakeview and Lincoln Square show where this one is headed.
